
# 1. Chief Financial Officer (CFO) – John Marshall

## Analytical Focus
Enterprise profitability, margin protection, and financial sustainability.

## Key Business Questions

- Which stores are truly profitable after discounts and cost of goods?
- How does gross margin vary by store and by day?
- Which products and categories drive the most profit?
- Are discounts eroding overall margin?
- Which stores generate high revenue but low profit?
- What is Profit After Labour by store?

## Decisions Supported

- Budget allocation
- Store-level intervention
- Cost reduction initiatives
- Margin improvement strategy

# Enterprise Impact

Collectively, these stakeholder questions define the analytical scope of the BI solution.

The data warehouse model must support:

- Store-level performance analysis
- Product-level profitability analysis
- Labour and waste cost visibility
- Channel and promotion effectiveness
- Standardised KPI definitions across departments

These requirements drive the design of the Bronze/Silver/Gold architecture and Power BI semantic model.
